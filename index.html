Background

Born into a family of entertainers in Sacramento, California, Jonathan James' parents were a traveling circus performer and a stage assistant. He moved around all the time as a child until he grew up surrounded by off-season trailers, fairs, and behind-the-scenes circus tents that made up America’s carnivals. At age four, James was well and truly entrenched in his family’s traveling circus. Instead of being schooled in regular sports and games, his father taught him some basics of sleight of hand, card tricks, and how to stand on stage. At age seven, Jane sat at the back of his father’s tent, observing the customers while he told their fortunes. He soon learned the skill of seeing what adults didn’t see—being able to differentiate between genuine distress and an act, finding the wedding ring tucked away in a pocket, and reading the body language of desperate souls seeking answers. At age ten, he started working on designing his own parlor tricks, knowing that people generally look where they are told and hear what they so badly wish to hear. Desiring a short-lived normalcy, Jane's mother made him attend a regular high school in northern California. Of course, James proved to be a very bad student in the traditional sense—he found himself often bored, attending class irregularly, and viewing school as an experiment in psychology. Instead of participating in sports or clubs, James found himself spending his lunch periods doing mind-reading experiments for his classmates, becoming adept at navigating the social strata of high school through the anxieties, secrets, and crushes of his classmates. Through cold reading, he managed to get out of detentions, convinced teachers to change his grades, and prevented fights from breaking out. However, by age seventeen, academics failed to keep him engaged anymore, even though he just went through the motions to complete his high school diploma at age eighteen, just to satisfy the formalities but still managing to achieve above 3.9 GPA. James left his house immediately the day after his graduation and embarked on his journey. His performances started in small-town venues where he performed solo acts that involved mentalism, hypnosis, and sleight of hand. It was during this period that he completely took up the role of being a prodigy, knowing how to captivate an audience, charm people, and make use of their superstitions. By the time James hit his mid-twenties, he was already past the stage of performing only for local theater audiences and private galas. From a simple carnival performer to a sophisticated TV show performer, James transformed himself into a world-renowned psychic and medium capable of “talking to the dead” using his witty tongue, Hollywood looks, and amazing cold-reading skills. This helped him get lucrative TV shows, write books, and earn a lot of money using the pain and curiosity of his rich clients and talk show viewers. At the height of his career, James found himself marrying Angela – an astute lady who could see through all the charm he exuded on the public platform and made him earthbound. Within the next twelve months, they welcomed a baby girl, named Charlotte. For some time, James tried to manage his hectic schedule of appearing on television shows amidst all the luxury in which they lived in Northern California. Though Angela was internally opposed to the deceitful business he was into, James paid no heed, sure of his invincibility. At twenty-seven years old, James had reached the peak of his career as well as his self-confidence. There was not a soul alive who he did not think he could outwit and manipulate. James made frequent appearances on broadcast television as an expert commentator who analyzed high-profile crimes and controversies in a manner of untouchable arrogance. James thought of himself as everyone’s audience without realizing that his celebrity status would attract the attention of a deadly, aberrant cell. James was twenty-eight years old when he hit his absolute pinnacle of fame through a prime-time TV special. In the midst of a live broadcast, a radical anomalous extremist group called the TCI tried to execute their terror attack by hacking into the international media channels and broadcasting the face of SCP-096. This hack did not work owing to an unexpected signal interference problem, and the public remained blissfully unaware of how narrowly they escaped the apocalyptic catastrophe. James, who was ignorant about the anomalous world and only knew that the TCI was some pathetic group trying to gain reputation through terrorism, spent his monologue mercilessly ridiculing them. Even smugly inviting them to "Try harder next time" Days later, James came back home after recording his album at the studio. On his way back home, he saw that the front door of the house was wide open and unlocked. There was a folded paper that had been nailed at eye-level on the front door. James pulled down the paper and unfolded it. It read: "Dear Jonathan, You were so entertaining on television. So clever. So confident in your little parlor tricks. You told the world we were a joke. You told your audience we couldn't hurt a soul. But you see, Jonathan... you don't possess psychic vision. You're just a greedy, arrogant little fraud. If you really were psychic, you wouldn't have to open the door to see what we've done to your lovely wife and daughter." Feeling dread creeping up on him, James did not dare to enter but instead switched on the lights and discovered that his house was quiet, covered in blood. Lying dead in the living room were his wife, Angela, and his daughter, Charlotte, who had been killed by a strike force of the TCI using unorthodox anomalous weapons. Above their dead bodies was painted the logo of the TCI in blood. Unable to cope with his guilty conscience and understanding that the source of his destruction was his inflated ego, James ended his showbiz career, severed all ties with the media, and withdrew from society altogether. Almost four years were spent by James hiding out in a seaside town, completely alone and refusing to do any more mentalism or cold reading, and almost two of them were unfortunately committed to a psychiatric hospital. All his time outside of the hospital was spent searching through police archives, public databases, and occult websites hoping to find any hint regarding the mark on the wall. James didn’t know that the Foundation was closing in on him.

Foundational Recruitment

In trying to locate cold hits relating to TCI attacks in online discussions, James inadvertently ran into a Foundation web crawler looking for GoI terms. As a result, the Intelligence Agency field team was dispatched upon recognizing a civilian that made some terrifyingly precise links between the TCI attacks and any other anomalies. His little apartment was searched, and he was expected to be found guilty of being some kind of secret memetic agent or reality bender. James was detained at a Foundation facility and had to go through several weeks of containment procedures and anomaly tests. James was able to neutralize the Lead Researcher during an interrogation by Foundation Researchers within the first three minutes using an explanation of his marital issues, gambling habits, and body language, clearly demonstrating that he did not have any anomalies at all but rather was exceptionally gifted at reading humans. Instead of giving James a Class-A amnestic that would erase the priceless behavioral information he had gathered using the TCI, the Regional IA Director realized an exceptional opportunity. James was given two options: to forget about his family's murderers for good using the amnestic, or to sign up as a Level 3 Intelligence Agency consultant in order to track down cults and GoI groups. James signed up to the contract immediately. For the following two years, he went through intensive training on Foundation security protocol, threat assessment, GoI systems and so on, fitting himself perfectly as IA’s most unique behavioral consultant. .

Documentation & Findings [IR-4001]

Summary: When the event took place, Consultant James was interrogating a captured GoI-004 agent under conditions of intense stress. Halfway through his question, the subject disappeared from sight, leaving behind only empty restraining straps falling onto the metal chair. At the same time, two out of three IA guards armed and present in the observation chamber disappeared as well. While most of the anomalies have an ambient hum or some sort of distortion in space when they manifest, James noted that this one did not. Exiting the interrogation room, James found that everything had stopped working. Almost three quarters of the staff working on security, administration, and research at the facility had disappeared in mid-step. Tactical equipment was left vacant on corridors; cups of steaming coffee were left overturned on desks by hands which no longer existed. In response to being questioned by Ethics Committee investigators about how he dealt with the shocking fact that billions of lives had been erased from his memory without leaving any trace, it is reported that James made a cup of tea, gazed out of the observation room window, and said: "The awful thing is not seeing people fade away into nothingness. The awful thing is realizing that three minutes ago, I could have had a brother, a role model, or a best friend... and now I don’t even have the memory of someone worth missing. Not only did the universe destroy their lives but took away my right to mourn them." When James was asked about his memories of his dead wife and daughter, he clearly stated that he still remembers that very well, visibly displeased by the question.
